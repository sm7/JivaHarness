# JivaHarness India fundraising strategy

**Decision:** raise a **₹1.5 crore pre-seed equity round** for an 18-month non-classified pilot plan while applying in parallel to the **iDEX Open Challenge for up to ₹1.5 crore**. Do not attempt a large institutional round yet.

**Working assumptions:** India private limited company; DPIIT eligibility; pre-revenue; one technical founder; the repository is the current product evidence. Replace these assumptions before external use.

## 1. Investable story

### One-line pitch

**JivaHarness is the identity, authorization, and audit control plane that keeps autonomous defence AI agents inside delegated authority and produces verifiable evidence of every action.**

### Initial wedge

Start with a non-kinetic, non-classified workflow: procurement, logistics, maintenance, or administrative decision support. The first pilot should prove that an agent can:

1. receive identity-scoped, time-limited authority;
2. be blocked outside policy or tool scope;
3. execute an approved action; and
4. produce a tamper-evident action record and authorization lineage.

This matches what the repository already demonstrates: a deterministic procurement action, scoped OBO credentials, policy evaluation, Citta identity continuity, and a Merkle proof. It avoids pretending that the current reference harness is already a deployable defence platform.

### Language to use

Lead with **mission assurance, delegated authority, zero-trust agent identity, policy enforcement, and audit evidence**. Explain the Jiva–Atman architecture only after the operational problem is clear. Do not lead with philosophy, consciousness, or a generic “responsible AI” claim.

### 18-month milestones

1. **Month 0–3:** incorporate, obtain DPIIT recognition, assign all IP to the company, submit iDEX, and secure two design-partner letters.

4. **Month 12–18:** convert one pilot to paid deployment, establish a commercial critical-infrastructure use case, and raise seed on evidence rather than architecture.

## 3. Capital stack and application order

### Apply now

1. **iDEX Open Challenge — highest priority.** It accepts founder-proposed technology rather than requiring a listed problem statement, which fits an agent-governance control plane. Submit by 30 September 2026.[1]
2. **DPIIT recognition — prerequisite path.** Needed for the strongest startup-program eligibility story and for the Startup India Seed Fund route.
3. **Startup India Seed Fund Scheme — backup, not the main plan.** The official criteria require DPIIT recognition, incorporation no more than two years before application, a technology-led scalable product, at least 51% Indian-promoter shareholding, and no more than ₹10 lakh of prior monetary support from another central/state scheme; defence is explicitly preferred.[3] Apply before accepting a larger government grant if you want to preserve eligibility.

### Apply after MVP/pilot evidence

4. **MeitY SAMRIDH.** It supports selected accelerators serving IT startups with acceleration plus one-to-one matching funding up to ₹40 lakh and focuses on customer, investor, and international-market access.[4] Use this when the product needs scale and market access, not as a substitute for the first defence use case.

## 5. Proof package before outreach

Build only these six items:

1. **10-slide deck:** problem, wedge, demo, architecture, why now, defence/customer path, dual-use market, milestones, team, round.
2. **Two-minute demo:** allowed action, denied action, expired credential, evidence proof.
3. **One-page iDEX proposal:** military problem, user, current readiness, 12-month prototype, test plan, budget, indigenous IP.
4. **One-page pilot offer:** non-classified data, 8–12 weeks, fixed success criteria, paid or letter-backed.
5. **Data room:** incorporation/DPIIT, cap table, founder IP assignment, budget, architecture, threat model, pilot letters.
6. **Evidence sheet:** tests currently passing, what is deterministic, what is not integrated, and the next validation milestone.

Skip a detailed five-year model. A monthly 18-month cash plan and milestone budget are sufficient before revenue evidence.

## 8. Stop/go gates

- **Do not raise institutional capital on the paper alone.** Get a user, integrated demo, or grant signal first.
- **Do not claim battlefield readiness, compliance, or live model governance.** The repository explicitly says those integrations are not yet implemented.
- **Do not accept a defence-only valuation story.** Preserve the same control plane for critical infrastructure, financial services, and regulated enterprise agents.
- **Do not add foreign investors or a foreign parent casually.** Before accepting foreign money, get Indian counsel to review FEMA/FDI, beneficial ownership, sector classification, grant eligibility, and defence/customer restrictions.
- **Do not pursue classified data in the first pilot.** Prove authorization and evidence quality on a non-classified workflow.
